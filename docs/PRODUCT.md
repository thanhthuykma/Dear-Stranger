# Dear Stranger

> **Because sometimes it's easier to talk to a stranger.**

## Overview

Dear Stranger is an anonymous peer support platform where people can safely share their thoughts, emotions, and life experiences without fear of being judged.

Instead of focusing on likes, followers, or popularity, Dear Stranger encourages genuine human connection through empathy, kindness, and understanding.

The goal is simple:

> No one should feel like they have to carry everything alone.

---

# The Problem

Many people struggle with stress, anxiety, loneliness, heartbreak, burnout, or family issues.

Although they want to talk, they often don't because they are afraid of:

- Being judged
- Burdening friends or family
- Feeling misunderstood
- Losing privacy

As a result, many choose to remain silent.

---

# Our Solution

Dear Stranger provides a safe space where people can:

- Share anonymously
- Read stories from others with similar experiences
- Receive empathy instead of judgment
- Feel understood by people who have been there

In the future, users may optionally connect with others who are open to conversation.

Just honest conversations between strangers.

# MVP

Version 1 focuses on the core experience.

### Authentication

- Google Sign-In
- Email & Password

### Anonymous Community

- Create anonymous posts
- Browse community feed
- View post details
- Anonymous comments

### Discovery

- Categories
- Search
- Filter

### User Profile

- Profile
- Saved posts
- Settings

### Safety

- Report content
- Block users
- Community Guidelines

### Notifications

- Replies
- Mentions (future)
- Match requests (future)

---

# Future Vision

Dear Stranger is more than a social platform.

Future versions may include:

- Anonymous matching
- One-to-one conversations
- Peer support circles
- AI-assisted journaling
- Mood tracking
- Wellness exercises

These features are intentionally excluded from the MVP to keep the first release focused.

---

# Success Criteria

The MVP is successful if users can:

- Create an account
- Share anonymously
- Read others' stories
- Receive supportive comments
- Feel safe using the platform

# Guiding Principle

Every feature should answer one question:

> **Does this help people feel safe enough to open up?**

If the answer is no, it does not belong in the MVP.
